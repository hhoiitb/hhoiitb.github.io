27 Oct 2025

🌊 IIT Bombay at India Maritime Week 2025 ⚓

Last week, faculty members and students from Ocean Engineering, IIT Bombay participated in the India Maritime Week 2025, held at the NESCO Exhibition Centre, Mumbai, from 27–31 October 2025, and engaged with a wide range of stakeholders, industry leaders, and international participants, contributing to discussions on maritime technology, coastal resilience, and sustainable ocean practices.

As part of the India Maritime Innovation Hub, the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay established its chapter and showcased its ongoing research projects, advanced laboratory facilities, and innovations in ocean and coastal engineering.

In line with the vision of our Hon’ble Prime Minister, IIT Bombay signed three Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) with the Government of Maharashtra to establish:

➡️ R&D Infrastructure for Ship Design and Ship Building at IIT Bombay

➡️ A Centre of Excellence (CoE) for capacity building and training of manpower in Ship Design and Ship Building (SDB)

➡️ An e-Postgraduate Diploma (e-PGD) program for Maharashtra Maritime Board (MMB) engineers.

These initiatives aim to strengthen India’s shipbuilding ecosystem through research, innovation, and skill development, reinforcing IIT Bombay’s commitment to advancing maritime education and technology.

It was also a great pleasure to host Shri S. Viswanathan, IAS, Deputy Chairman, Chennai Port and Managing Director, Maritime India Foundation, along with his team, at the Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Bombay, on 30 October 2025.

During his visit, we showcased the Department’s expertise in Maritime and Ocean Engineering and explored opportunities for collaboration to further strengthen India’s maritime research and innovation landscape.

🌊 Understanding the Impact of Tropical Cyclones on Coral Reefs in the Great Barrier Reef

As part of the IAHR YPN Western India gathering, we had the privilege of attending a talk by Prof. Ananth W., who shared his pioneering work experience along with a team of multi-disciplinary researchers at The University of Queensland on how tropical cyclones affect coral reef systems, focusing on the Great Barrier Reef, Australia.

GROW-N is now a huge community of professionals, researchers, scientists, professors and students from remote sensing, ocean engineering and water resources. While GROW has now expanded to become an online platform, we are still an active community within Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. We have a team of enthusiastic students who volunteer to conduct in-person GROW-N activities to bring us all together from time to time. With the new academic year, we also have a new set of students who came forward to organize an orientation program. We had an opportunity to share some insights on why we need a community like GROW-N and how all of us, especially students can benefit from this community. We are looking forward to this year's activities that the new team have planned.

We had the privilege of hosting Dr. Velamala Simhadri Naidu, Former Senior Principal Scientist at CSIR–National Institute of Oceanography, Mumbai, for an insightful seminar on “Coastal Development and Its Impact on Marine Environment.” Dr. Naidu shared his vast expertise on environmental impact assessments, coastal regulation, effluent disposal modeling and the challenges of balancing infrastructure growth with marine ecosystem sustainability. His career journey, ranging from developing the buoyant jet model to training scientists and contributing to CRZ clearances, has truly inspired the participants.

Looking forward to the opportunities!
